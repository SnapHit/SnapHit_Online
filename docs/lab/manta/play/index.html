Raywake keeps nothing about you, apart from any feedback you choose to send. Playing online, your phone sends the room your name, your steering, the part of the ocean you are looking at and a random key, kept in this tab, that lets you rejoin after a dropped connection; the room forgets it all when it empties. Your name, your best, your sound setting and the day you last played stay on this phone. A private room's code is kept until it has had no players for 24 hours, and the address that asked for it for 10 seconds, to stop floods. The game counts plays (visits, runs, room joins, runs past a minute, and whether you have played on an earlier day) with nothing that identifies you: no address, no browser, no name. Feedback is kept without your address; to allow three messages a day, the server holds a scrambled form of the address, which cannot be turned back into it, in memory for that day only. No accounts, no cookies, nothing from anyone else's servers. The site runs on Cloudflare, which keeps its own short-lived connection logs.

Raywake stores only your best and your sound setting on this device, and contacts no server: it plays entirely from the files in this package. No accounts, no cookies, no analytics.
