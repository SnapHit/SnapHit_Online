Manta trains keeps nothing about you. Playing online, your phone sends the room only your steering, the part of the ocean you are looking at and a random key, kept in this tab, that lets you rejoin after a dropped connection. The room gives you a random name and forgets it all when it empties. A private room's code is kept until it has had no players for 24 hours, and the address that asked for it for 10 seconds, to stop floods. No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, nothing from anyone else's servers. The site runs on Cloudflare, which keeps its own short-lived connection logs.
